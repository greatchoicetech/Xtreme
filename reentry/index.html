Behind the wire

Reentry

The program is built so a man is ready on the day the sentence ends, and so he is not alone the week after.

After the gate

Because of community partners, we have helped many men after their end of sentence with clothing, tools, work boots, bicycles, rent assistance, food, pastoral counseling, employment referrals, and ordinary support.

Sheridan Correa

Sheridan was radically changed by Jesus and now lives free of mental illness, addiction, incarceration, and broken relationships. He is a biblical counselor who leads others toward freedom, transformation, and healing.

Great time sitting down to lunch with this success story. He has been out for almost 4 years and is doing everything right. He has a great job, is looking at buying a house soon, and is giving back by helping his fellow man in times of need. So proud of his accomplishments and of hearing how he is encouraging and lifting others up.
