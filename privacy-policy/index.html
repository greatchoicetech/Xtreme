Privacy policy

This site is a static website for Xtreme SOULutions, PO Box 5487, Ocala, FL 34478. Phone (352) 694-4888.

What this site stores

The pages themselves do not have accounts, comments, or a database. The contact form opens your own email app with the note you typed, and you send it from that email account. It is not transmitted to a Xtreme SOULutions server, because there is not one behind these pages.

Donations and wishlists

Gifts made through Zeffy, and items bought from the Amazon wishlist, are handled by those companies under their own privacy policies. We receive the gift or the item, not your card number.

Video and social media

Films from WUFT and the Sheridan Correa video play from YouTube only after you press play. A link to Facebook leaves this site. Those services may set their own cookies once you open them.

Analytics

The site loads Google Analytics so we can see which pages are used. You can block that with your browser's tracking controls.

Registration

Shoot-out registration is collected by Jotform on its own form, not by this website.

Your requests

To ask what we hold about you from a phone call, a gift, or a message you sent us, call (352) 694-4888. We will not delete records we are required to keep for legal, accounting, or security reasons.
