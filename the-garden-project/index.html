Behind the wire

The Garden Project

Work you can see. Food you can give away. A classroom that happens to be made of dirt.

The Garden Project began in April 2022 on three-quarters of an acre. Fifty-eight men prepared the ground, installed irrigation, and germinated seeds. The garden is organic and uses natural means to fight pests, fertilize, and regenerate the soil.

The first harvest in 2022 yielded more than 400 summer squash. Some went to a local shelter. Some were cooked for the gardeners.

The administration then added another acre and a quarter and opened the project to the whole compound, so men outside the program work beside the Xtreme SOULutions crew.

By the end of 2024 the garden had given more than 24,000 pounds of produce to local shelters, and healthy food back to the men who grew it.
