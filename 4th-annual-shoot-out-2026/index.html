Shoot Out at Blackjack's

A morning of sporting clays and fellowship for the benefit of Xtreme SOULutions. The date announced for this event was Friday, April 3, 2026.

  • Date: Friday, April 3, 2026
  • Registration and coffee: 8:00 a.m.
  • Shotgun start: 9:00 a.m.
  • Awards luncheon: 11:30 a.m.

Blackjack Sporting Clays
3372 County Road 526
Sumterville, FL 33585

All ages are welcome. Guests under 18 must be with an adult. No handguns or rifles.

Registration includes one golf cart per team. Each person gets 100 targets, 100 rounds of ammunition, eye and ear protection, and a barbecue lunch. Shooters bring their own 12 or 20 gauge shotgun.

Individuals may register for $300 and be placed on a team if there is room. See previous shoot outs.
