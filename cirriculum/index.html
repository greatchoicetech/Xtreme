Behind the wire

Curriculum

Twenty-one core classes. Twelve weeks each. A long enough runway to practice a different life before the gate opens.

  • Participants take three classes each semester and attend weekly family and wing meetings and monthly community meetings. Celebration Sunday services are voluntary.
  • Completion usually takes about 2 and a half to 3 years, depending on availability and aptitude.
  • Instructor and leadership training are additional paths.
  • Gifts in writing, art, management, and administration are assigned real work. Artists paint murals on the compound.
  • We coordinate with Classification, Education, and the TIER program.
  • The core curriculum was accredited in 2018 by the FDC clearinghouse, and graduation is noted on the FDC screen.

Food handling

Over the years we have trained and certified more than 50 food handlers and 10 food managers through the National Food Safety Association. Certifications are valid for five years, so a man can walk out ready to work.

The garden, as a classroom

In 2022 we began a garden on an unused lot behind the vocation building. Fifty-eight men prepared the ground, installed irrigation, germinated seeds, and harvested vegetables. Some produce went to the MCI kitchen. Some was given to local shelters. Local business owners, individual donors, and a grant from Serving USA funded the start.

Phase two brought 1,500 sweet potato plants, 400 yellow squash plants, and a community garden of tomatoes, lettuce, peanuts, watermelon, and peppers. At harvest the men learn how to prepare, store, and serve what they grew. The garden has its own page.

What partners have supplied

  • Air conditioners for F Dorm dayrooms
  • Televisions and DVD players
  • Art supplies for murals throughout the compound
  • Sand for the volleyball court
  • 140 office chairs for staff at MCI
  • Clothing for the warehouse, including unusual sizes and clothes for men at the end of sentence

Partners have included the Community Foundation for Ocala Marion County, Salt Life Church, Sonny's BBQ, Christ's Church of Marion County, Pay It Forward Outreach, and many others.

After release

We have helped men with clothing, tools, work boots, bicycles, rent assistance, food, pastoral counseling, employment referrals, and general support.

Xtreme SOULutions guides men from prison life toward restoration, family, work, and a purpose-filled life in Marion County.
