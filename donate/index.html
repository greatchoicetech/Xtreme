Every person is made in the image of God, and no life is beyond His reach.

Xtreme SOULutions offers incarcerated men a way to grow that restores dignity. That change reaches the people around them, not only the man in the class.

Your gift is not only for this month. It builds a community with moral footing, financial stability, and hope for the next generation.

A gift of any size is put to work: curriculum, the garden, and the practical help a man needs when he leaves.

Donate on Zeffy Amazon wishlist

Zeffy is the donation form. The wishlist sends specific items straight to the needs we have listed. If something more urgent comes up, such as curriculum or DVDs, contact us and we will tell you.
