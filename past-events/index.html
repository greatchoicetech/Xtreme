Past shoot outs

Teams, barbecue, and a short account of the work inside Marion Correctional Institution.

4th Annual · March 20, 2025

The day began with the sunrise and clear weather. Sonny's BBQ served lunch while Jeremiah Bennett told his story of volunteering inside the prison with Xtreme SOULutions.

First place: The Boyd Group. Second: Benmar. Third: RoMac.

Presenting sponsors: First Orlando and Sonny's BBQ. Silver: Benmar Construction, RoMac Building Supplies, the City of Ocala, Cepra Landscaping, Turf Solutions, Harrell's Fertilizer, Ferrentino and Sons, and The Boyd Group. Also: Chad's Water Works, and Landscape Sod and Beyond.

3rd Annual · March 22, 2024

The morning opened under a severe-weather warning. Shooters arrived in raincoats. After prayer, the clouds moved around the course and the morning stayed dry.

First place: Sonny's BBQ. Second: Boyd Real Estate Development. Third: Cepra Landscaping, Turf Solutions, and Harrell's Fertilizer.

Presenting: Admiral Furniture and Boyd Real Estate. Silver: Bailey's Building and Construction, Benmar Construction, Chad's Water Works, John Rankin Construction, RoMac Building Supplies, Senator Keith Perry, The Zone, and Triple Crown Trailers. Also: Cepra Landscaping, CIC Site Development, Harrell's Fertilizer, JDR Leadership, and Turf Solutions.

2nd Annual · October 27, 2023

A warm, sunny day and 34 participants, then pulled pork from Sonny's BBQ.

First: Sonny's BBQ. Second: Your Home Sold Guaranteed Realty. Third: Podcast Army.

Presenting: Admiral Furniture and Boyd Real Estate. Silver: Benmar Construction, Big D Roofing, Keith Perry for State Senate, Kevin McDonald Agency (Allstate), Perry's Hill Bulldozing, Podcast Army, SolarTrek, Sonny's BBQ, Triple Crown Trailers, and Your Home Sold Guaranteed. Also: JDR Leadership and SWAT.

1st Annual · October 20, 2022

About 40 people gathered at Blackjack's Sporting Clays in Sumterville on a cold morning. That was the start of the tradition: teams of four, a clay-pigeon trophy, and lunch no matter the weather.

First: Sonny's BBQ. Second: Your Home Sold Guaranteed Realty. Third: Triple Crown Trailers.

Presenting: Admiral Furniture, Bedrock Resources, Boyd Real Estate, Maricamping LLC, and Triple Crown Trailers. Silver: Crossties of Ocala, Eaton's Ski Beach, Keith Perry for State Senate, Perry's Hill Bulldozing Service, Stentiford Construction, The McDonald Agency (Allstate), and Your Home Sold Guaranteed Realty. Also: Alvarez Trucking, Big D Roofing, Black Label Marine Group, Carl Heise Construction, John Rankin Construction, and The Zone Health and Fitness.

The tradition

Freezing cold, a forecast tropical storm, or hot sun, the teams still show up. Lunch is pulled pork, slaw, and macaroni and cheese from Sonny's BBQ. President Blaine Whitt and CEO Kimberly Whitt tell what Xtreme SOULutions is for. Senior staff from Marion Correctional Institution, including wardens and colonels, have said this is the most effective program they have seen in many years in the prison system.

We are grateful to the board, the local businesses that sponsor the day, and the donors who make it work.
