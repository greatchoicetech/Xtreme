Be a Kingdom influencer

A peer-to-peer fundraiser does not have to be a second job.

If you post on Facebook, Instagram, X, TikTok, or your own website, you can occasionally mention the work Xtreme SOULutions is doing and invite people to support it. A personal story is powerful. If you do not have one, tell one of the stories already on this site.

How to start

  1. Read a reentry story or the story of the ministry.
  2. Share it in your own words, with a link to this site.
  3. Point people to the Zeffy donation form. Zeffy does not take a platform fee from the gift.

You can be an influencer for the Kingdom of God by telling others about the life-changing work happening with incarcerated men in Marion County.
